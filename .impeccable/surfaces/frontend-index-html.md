## Scope

The SpaceForge web frontend: landing (Persuade) plus the Forge studio, Catalog and Design sheet (Operate). Runs on a mock API layer until the backend exists.

## Audience and job

Builders and developers (primary) outline a plot they have the landowner's permission for, forge a priced design and publish it. Dreamers forge privately and share by link. Buyers browse listings and message the builder who forged a design.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every design earns its permit. The sheet's title block is an inspection card whose cells are stamped as the site is surveyed, the house briefed, forged, estimated and, with the landowner's sign-off, published. It refuses the real-estate hero over a listing grid and the AI prompt box on a gradient.

OWN-WORLD: A blue-black drafting room; lit cyanotype sheets with white linework are the only bright objects; molten amber only on stamps, the forge and the one primary action. Zodiak hairline capitals for display, Hanken Grotesk for everything practical, tabular figures. Square corners, registered layers (site, plan, structure, cost) toggled like print passes.

STORY: A visitor watches a real plot become a priced house in one viewport, believes the land shaped it, and outlines their own site.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 47%: a sticky lit sheet with a site forging into a house (Rive) and its inspection card. Right: one sentence and the amber "Outline a site" action. The lowercase wordmark runs along the bottom, crossing the sheet.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
